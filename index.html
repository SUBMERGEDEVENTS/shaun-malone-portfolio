Brands Founded

Cumulus Entertainment
2011 — 2020 · Cleveland, OH
Founded from scratch in 2011, Cumulus Entertainment became one of Cleveland's premier electronic music promotion companies over a decade of operation. The Agora Theater served as the primary home for flagship productions including Radiate, Disco of the Dead, Winter Warm Up, and Gamma. Alongside The Agora, Cumulus built deep partnerships with House of Blues, Beachland Ballroom (where Shaun also served as Production Manager and Talent Buyer 2014–2017), and Jacobs Pavilion. Responsible for bringing major national touring acts — including Skrillex, Bassnectar, Zedd, Excision, Major Lazer, Kaskade, and Paul Oakenfold — to Cleveland audiences. Total reach: 100,000+ attendees across 10 years and 300+ events.
SUB:MERGED Events
2021 — Present · Cleveland / Akron, OH
Launched in 2021 as the successor to Cumulus, SUB:MERGED Events expanded the model into deeper bass music, house, techno, and drum & bass programming. Built ongoing series including SUB:SONIC, Rinse & Repeat, Rooftop Rumble, and Summer Rave. Cultivated partnerships with Mercury Music Lounge, Musica, and The Winchester as primary venues, while continuing to book at The Agora for major touring acts. Currently active through 2026 with consistent monthly programming.
